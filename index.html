Your practitioner

Every first visit starts with a conversation.

Mayumi Arahata has worked in massage and seitai for 18 years, and in Australia since 2007. She holds massage qualifications in both Japan and Australia, and studied with Dr Ken Yamamoto (KYT technique) and Dr Makoto Minami (traditional Japanese therapy).

Before opening her own clinics she was sponsored by The Spa at the Observatory Hotel, now The Langham, Sydney.

Your first visit

What happens, from booking to payment.

  1. Book online

    Choose a treatment and a time on Fresha. Your booking is confirmed straight away.

  2. Arrive five minutes early

    On a first visit there is a short consultation form to fill in.

  3. Talk, then treatment

    We go through what's bothering you before we start. Sessions finish at the booked time, so a late start means a shorter treatment.

  4. Pay and claim

    Cash, card, Apple Pay and Google Pay. Remedial massage can be claimed on the spot through HICAPS.

Using private health cover

Remedial and sports massage can be claimed if your policy includes remedial massage. We process the claim through HICAPS when you pay, so you only pay the difference. Please bring your health fund card.
