Learn massage

Learn massage from a therapist who still treats every day.

Mayumi teaches alongside her clinic work: a 90-minute workshop for complete beginners, and one-to-one lessons for people who want to get better. In English or Japanese.

For complete beginners

OMOIYARI massage workshop

A relaxed 90 minutes for anyone who has thought "I'd like to try massage". You work in pairs, giving and receiving, on the hands, forearms and shoulders. The aim is not to memorise techniques. It is to feel what touch does.

  • Stroking, kneading, tapping, holding: how the way you touch changes the result
  • Dry massage compared with oil or cream
  • Why firmer is not always better, and how the same touch feels different to different people
  • Using your hands, arms and body so that you do not tire
  • Checking pressure and putting the other person at ease
Length
90 minutes
